// 01 — Sizing

Boxy, true, size up for layers.

// 02 — Care

Wear it into the ground.

Heavyweight cotton softens with every wash. Treat it carefully and you'll have it for a decade — that's the whole point of buying one of these instead of ten of something else.

// Wash · Cotton + knit

Cold. Inside out.

30°C maximum. Inside out preserves the screen-prints and the signed hem. Mild detergent — no fabric softener, no bleach. Wash with similar weights.

  • Cold wash · max 30°C
  • No bleach · no softener
  • No tumble dry · ever
// Dry · Air only

Lay flat. Wait.

Hang knits flat to dry — they stretch lengthwise if hung wet. Tees can hang from a wood hanger by the seam. Shell jackets line-dry away from direct sun.

  • Knit · flat dry
  • Tee · hang by seam
  • Shell · shade · 24h
// Iron · Low only

Inverse, low.

Iron inside out, low heat. For the quilted shell, steam from 6 inches away — never press the chambers. Knits don't need iron — steam them on the hanger.

  • Iron inverse · low
  • Quilted · steam only
  • Knit · hanger steam
~ 10 yrs

If a seam goes, mail it back. We repair anything we made for the first three years free, and at cost after that. Patches, button replacements, knit re-blocking, hem re-stitch — we do it in the Berlin studio on the first Tuesday of every month.
